## 1. Federal Contract "Set-Asides" (The Massive Hidden Market)
The US federal government is the world's largest buyer of goods and services, and by law, it must allocate a specific percentage of contracts to small, disadvantaged businesses. * 
* SBA 8(a) Business Development Program: This is a 9-year program run by the [Small Business Administration (SBA)](https://www.sba.gov/). It provides intensive mentoring and allows you to compete for sole-source contracts capped at up to $4 million for services. [1, 2] 
* Minority Business Enterprise (MBE) Certification: Issued via the [National Minority Supplier Development Council (NMSDC)](https://nmsdc.org/), this acts as your passport to corporate America. Fortune 500 companies use the NMSDC database to fulfill their supplier diversity quotas, buying everything from corporate art installations to tech infrastructure. [1, 2] 
* Disadvantaged Business Enterprise (DBE): Primarily used for state and local government municipal contracts (e.g., public art projects, transit tech, municipal websites). [1]
