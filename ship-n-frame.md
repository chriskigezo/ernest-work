You build the brand, the website, and the customer acquisition engine. The customer uploads an image or requests a mail-in kit. ## The Dual-Engine Business Model
You will operate a hybrid platform that monetizes both the demand side (consumers/artists) and the supply side (local shops) without owning a single piece of framing equipment.

## Engine A: The Lead Generation Playbook

* The Flow: High-intent users (artists, interior designers, homeowners) visit your site searching for framing services or pricing calculators. They fill out a multi-step form detailing their project size, material choices, and ZIP code.
* The Monetization: You instantly route this qualified intent data to 3–4 local shops in your directory. Shops pay you a flat $10 to $35 per lead, regardless of whether they close the deal.

## Engine B: The B2B Affiliate Playbook

* The Flow: Local framers sign up to be listed in your directory to receive your leads. You become their trusted advisor for operational scaling.
* The Monetization: You feature affiliate links for industry-standard framing Point of Sale (POS) and workflow software (e.g., LifeSaver Software, FrameReady). When a shop signs up through your portal, you collect recurring SaaS affiliate revenue.

## Minimum-Cost Phase-1 Entry Plan (Bootstrap Budget)
To keep your operational costs near zero and grow naturally as your online presence builds, you will build a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) using a highly efficient tech stack:
## 1. Technical Setup & Initial Asset Build (Month 1)

* Website Platform: Build using WordPress (with an elementor or lightweight theme) or Webflow. Focus on a clean, professional aesthetic.
* Lead Capture & Directory Tools: Use Feathery or Typeform for the multi-step quote form. Use a basic database plugin to store local shop data.
* Initial Directory Scraping: Manually curate a list of the top 500 to 1,000 independent frame shops across the top 50 US metro areas. List them for free initially to populate your digital storefront.

## 2. Programmatic Local SEO Architecture (Months 2–3)
Do not write generic blog posts. Build an infrastructure focused entirely on transaction-oriented local landing pages:

* Geographic Pillars: Create landing pages matching the pattern: custom-framing-[city]-[state] (e.g., custom-framing-chicago-il).
* High-Intent Search Terms: Optimize these pages for keywords like "jersey framing near me," "fine art canvas stretching [City]," and "oversized poster framing."
* The Pricing Hook: Build a simple frontend "United Inches Calculator" tool. This acts as a lead-magnet, prompting users to input their dimensions to get a local cost estimate.
